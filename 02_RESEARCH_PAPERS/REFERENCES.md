# Research References

Six papers form the initial research set for FLYDRIVE's next design phase. This index records source details only; it does not contain paper summaries or design conclusions.

1. **BAXTER: Bi-modal Aerial-Terrestrial Hybrid Vehicle for Long-endurance Versatile Mobility** — Choi et al., 2021 preprint. [arXiv:2102.02942](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.02942). Supplied filename: `2102.02942v1.pdf`.
2. **Roller-Quadrotor: A Novel Hybrid Terrestrial/Aerial Quadrotor with Unicycle-Driven and Rotor-Assisted Turning** — Zheng et al., 2023 preprint. [arXiv:2303.00668](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.00668). Supplied filename: `2303.00668v3.pdf`.
3. **Design and Control of a Reconfigurable Robot with Rolling and Flying Locomotion** — Chang et al., *Actuators*, 2024. [https://doi.org/10.3390/act13010027](https://doi.org/10.3390/act13010027). Supplied filename: `actuators-13-00027.pdf`.
4. **Design and Experimental Validation of HyTAQ, a Hybrid Terrestrial and Aerial Quadrotor** — Kalantari and Spenko, ICRA 2013. [https://doi.org/10.1109/ICRA.2013.6631208](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICRA.2013.6631208). Supplied filename: `kalantari2013.pdf`.
5. **Quadrolltor: A Reconfigurable Quadrotor With Controlled Rolling and Turning** — Jia et al., *IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters*, 2023. [https://doi.org/10.1109/LRA.2023.3279624](https://doi.org/10.1109/LRA.2023.3279624). Supplied filename: `Quadrolltor-A-Reconfigurable-Quadrotor-With-Controlled-Rolling-and-Turning.pdf`.
6. **Beyond Conventional Drones: A Review of Unconventional Rotary-Wing UAV Design** — Li, *Drones*, 2025. [https://doi.org/10.3390/drones9050323](https://doi.org/10.3390/drones9050323). Supplied filename: `drones-09-00323-v2.pdf`.

The six PDFs were provided as project attachments for this update, but **PDF copies are not included in this Git commit**. Use the source links above to access the papers in the meantime. Add locally supplied PDF copies to this folder only after checking each source's license and redistribution terms.

These are external research references, not original FLYDRIVE work.
