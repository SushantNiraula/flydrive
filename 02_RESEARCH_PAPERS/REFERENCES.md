# Research References

Six papers form the initial research set for FLYDRIVE's next design phase. This index records the sources only; it does not contain paper summaries or design conclusions.

1. **BAXTER: Bi-modal Aerial-Terrestrial Hybrid Vehicle for Long-endurance Versatile Mobility** — Choi et al., 2021 preprint. [arXiv:2102.02942](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.02942). Local filename: `2102.02942v1.pdf`.
2. **Roller-Quadrotor: A Novel Hybrid Terrestrial/Aerial Quadrotor with Unicycle-Driven and Rotor-Assisted Turning** — Zheng et al., 2023 preprint. [arXiv:2303.00668](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.00668). Local filename: `2303.00668v3.pdf`.
3. **Design and Control of a Reconfigurable Robot with Rolling and Flying Locomotion** — Chang et al., *Actuators*, 2024. [https://doi.org/10.3390/act13010027](https://doi.org/10.3390/act13010027). Local filename: `actuators-13-00027.pdf`.
4. **Design and Experimental Validation of HyTAQ, a Hybrid Terrestrial and Aerial Quadrotor** — Kalantari and Spenko, ICRA 2013. Local filename: `kalantari2013.pdf`.
5. **Quadrolltor: A Reconfigurable Quadrotor With Controlled Rolling and Turning** — Jia et al., *IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters*, 2023. [https://doi.org/10.1109/LRA.2023.3279624](https://doi.org/10.1109/LRA.2023.3279624). Local filename: `Quadrolltor-A-Reconfigurable-Quadrotor-With-Controlled-Rolling-and-Turning.pdf`.
6. **Beyond Conventional Drones: A Review of Unconventional Rotary-Wing UAV Design** — Li, *Drones*, 2025. [https://doi.org/10.3390/drones9050323](https://doi.org/10.3390/drones9050323). Local filename: `drones-09-00323-v2.pdf`.

The papers are external research references, not original FLYDRIVE work. Check each source's license and redistribution terms before sharing PDF copies.
