# Research References

Six papers form the initial research set for FLYDRIVE's next design phase. This index records citations and links only; it does not contain paper summaries or design conclusions. DOI links identify the scholarly record; arXiv links lead to the preprint records where available.

1. **BAXTER: Bi-modal Aerial-Terrestrial Hybrid Vehicle for Long-endurance Versatile Mobility** — Choi et al., 2021 preprint. [DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2102.02942](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2102.02942) · [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.02942).
2. **Roller-Quadrotor: A Novel Hybrid Terrestrial/Aerial Quadrotor with Unicycle-Driven and Rotor-Assisted Turning** — Zheng et al., IROS 2023. [DOI: 10.1109/IROS55552.2023.10341703](https://doi.org/10.1109/IROS55552.2023.10341703) · [arXiv preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.00668).
3. **Design and Control of a Reconfigurable Robot with Rolling and Flying Locomotion** — Chang et al., *Actuators*, 2024. [DOI: 10.3390/act13010027](https://doi.org/10.3390/act13010027).
4. **Design and Experimental Validation of HyTAQ, a Hybrid Terrestrial and Aerial Quadrotor** — Kalantari and Spenko, ICRA 2013. [DOI: 10.1109/ICRA.2013.6631208](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICRA.2013.6631208).
5. **Quadrolltor: A Reconfigurable Quadrotor With Controlled Rolling and Turning** — Jia et al., *IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters*, 2023. [DOI: 10.1109/LRA.2023.3279624](https://doi.org/10.1109/LRA.2023.3279624).
6. **Beyond Conventional Drones: A Review of Unconventional Rotary-Wing UAV Design** — Li, *Drones*, 2025. [DOI: 10.3390/drones9050323](https://doi.org/10.3390/drones9050323).

The supplied PDFs are not mirrored in this repository. Use the DOI or source links to reach the publisher or repository landing pages. This avoids redistributing copies where the repository's rights are unclear and keeps large files out of Git history. The references are external research, not original FLYDRIVE work.
